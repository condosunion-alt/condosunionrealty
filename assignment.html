How It Works

What Is an Assignment Sale?

An assignment sale occurs when the original buyer of a pre-construction property sells their contract to a new buyer before the building is completed and the unit is occupied.

🔑

1. Original Buyer

Purchased at pre-construction pricing (often 2-3 years ago) and now needs to sell due to changed circumstances.

📋

2. Assignment Contract

The original contract is transferred to you — you take over the deposit and the purchase price.

🏠

3. You Take Ownership

Close on the closing date and receive the keys — often at a price below current market value.

Why Assignments

Benefits of Buying an Assignment

💰

Below-Market Pricing

Assignments often sell for 10-20% below current market value — because the original price was locked in years ago.

⏰

Move-In Sooner

Many assignments are in near-completion buildings — you could move in within months, not years.

✅

Verified Property

Unlike new pre-construction, you can see the actual unit, floor plan, and building progress before buying.

📝

Less Risky

The original buyer has already gone through status certificate review — less unknowns than buying direct from developer.
